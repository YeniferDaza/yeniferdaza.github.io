    Multiple product lines, from design intent to fabrication

    Driftwood · ready-to-assemble furniture

    About the project

    Multiple product lines, from design intent to fabrication

    Driftwood · ready-to-assemble furniture

    What the project was

    Product development for a multiple-line furniture catalogue: market research and product direction at the front, then 3D modelling, renders, packaging and fabrication drawings, produced to one standard per product code.

    The challenge

    The market was compact social-interest housing, so products had to be designed for small rooms and assembled by the customer — and designed the same way by several designers at once.

    My contribution

    • Researched the market and set the product direction — what to develop, for whom, and under what constraints
    • Modelled the products in 3D and defined the complete set each one had to arrive with
    • Defined four product-development criteria and trained a junior designer on the same framework
    • Handed over the highest-volume line and continued coordinating the catalogue remotely from Canada

    The result

    A defined product direction carried through to manufacture, developed to one standard across the catalogue — and a handover that held after I left the building.

    02

    Product and patterns redesigned for direct injection

    Calzado 3025 · Independent consultant

    Product and patterns redesigned for direct-injection manufacture, and a new women’s safety-footwear line developed from user research through to pilot production.

    About the project

    Challenge

    Direct injection is unforgiving: the upper has to be cut and assembled within tolerances the mould accepts, or the defect appears at the last operation, once all the material is spent.

    My contribution

    • Redesigned the product and its patterns for injection construction, iterating the pattern set across three versions
    • Wrote the technical and process specifications, under version control
    • Trained the production teams and ran pilot lots into series
    • Developed a new women’s safety-footwear line: user research, materials, requirements and prototypes through to pilot production

    Result

    Specifications in force with a version number and an effective date, and the pattern set stable at version 3.

    03

    Brands taken from product idea to manufacture

    Independent consultant · footwear and accessories

    Turning product ideas into something that could be made: requirements, hands-on modelling and prototyping, costed consumption and pilot runs.

    About the project

    Challenge

    A reference image is not something a factory can quote. Each client needed a specification, a costed consumption and a supplier who would schedule a small first run.

    My contribution

    • Turned client intent into a technical specification
    • Built the costed consumption sheet the brand prices from
    • Modelled and prototyped by hand, carrying each correction into the pilot run
    • Coordinated sourcing, production orders and delivery

    Result

    Products specified, costed, manufactured and shipped to schedule for more than one client.

  4. 05

    A geodesic dome taken through early-stage development

    Driftwood

    Design research and concept development taken through geometry and construction logic to two costed kit variants. Early-stage; not commercialised.

    About the project

    Challenge

    A ready-to-assemble geodesic dome had to be taken from an idea to something that could be costed and manufactured as a kit — including the regulatory and waterproofing questions that decide whether it is viable at all.

  5. Selected evidence

    06

    One manufacturing cost, resolved into five channel prices

    Driftwood

    Cost analysis at parts level — board yield, edge banding, hardware, packaging — so material and construction decisions could be priced while the product was still being designed.

    About the project

    Challenge

    The cost of a product depends on board yield, edge banding, hardware and packaging — and each sales route then takes a different commission, logistics cost and tax treatment out of it.

    My contribution

    • Built the itemised cost model per product code, at parts level
    • Built the per-channel price scenarios on that one cost base
    • Maintained the models across the range and across two years

    Result

    A costing and pricing structure applied across the range — the same method I had used six years earlier in footwear.
