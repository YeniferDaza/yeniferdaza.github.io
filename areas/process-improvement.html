  1. Featured project

    Diagnosed, redesigned, documented, trained — and left running

    Calzado 3025 · Independent consultant

    A footwear manufacturer’s product would not run consistently on the floor. The brief looked like a product problem and was a process one, so the deliverable was neither a drawing nor a report but a way of working the floor could keep.

    About the project

    Diagnosed, redesigned, documented, trained — and left running

    Calzado 3025 · Independent consultant

    What the project was

    Product redesigned for direct-injection construction, with the technical and process specifications written under version control, the production teams trained on them, pilot lots carried into series production, and a stage-by-stage non-conformance check put in place.

    The challenge

    The failure showed at the last operation, when all the material had been spent, and its causes were upstream — in the patterns, in specifications that described the product rather than how to make it, and in a practice that differed by operator.

    My contribution

    • Diagnosed at the operation, and redesigned the product and patterns for injection
    • Wrote the technical and process specifications, versioned and dated
    • Trained the production teams and corrected the model against pilot lots
    • Helped integrate the design process into the manufacturer’s quality management system

    The result

    Specifications in force with a version number and an effective date, a new product line developed, and non-conformance checked stage by stage on the floor.

  3. 04

    An ordering process modelled, documented and run for thirteen months

    Driftwood

    The function was left as artifacts rather than as knowledge in one head — a supplier database, a labelling system and a process model.

    About the project

    Challenge

    Production ran to order while despatch ran daily, so supply had to stay ahead of the workshop without tying up capital in timber.

    My contribution

    • Owned the ordering cycle, supplier to warehouse receipt
    • Produced the cut specifications and ran the optimisation
    • Built the supplier database and the stock labelling the function ran on
    • Modelled the process so it could be handed over and run the same way

    Result

    Thirteen consecutive months documented without a gap, and a transferable function.

  4. 05

    Sales and operations data turned into something a decision could be made from

    Driftwood

    Measurement is the part of process work that usually goes missing — this is where ordering, range and channel decisions got their figures.

    About the project

    Challenge

    Decisions about what to reorder, what to promote and what to stop making were being taken from impression rather than from a consistent set of figures.

    My contribution

    • Tracked and analysed sales and operations data across the range
    • Prepared the figures behind ordering, costing and channel decisions
    • Used the analysis to inform inventory, promotion and phase-out choices

    Result

    Range and ordering decisions taken against figures. The company’s wider reporting estate was built and maintained by a team.
