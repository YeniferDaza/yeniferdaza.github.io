Operations & Supply

Material supply for a made-to-order workshop

I ran material ordering and supplier coordination for a furniture manufacturer despatching daily — from the purchase decision through to the cutting specification the workshop worked from.

Driftwood Product & Operations Coordinator April 2021 – April 2022 Project overview

Context

Production ran to order while despatch ran daily. Supply had to stay ahead of the workshop without tying up capital in timber, and every board ordered had to arrive with a cutting specification that both the workshop and the external cutting service could work from.

My contribution

  1. Owned the ordering cycle end to end — purchase decisions, supplier coordination, delivery and warehouse receipt — continuously across thirteen months.
  2. Produced the cutting specifications and ran the optimisation in the format the external cutting service required, closing the loop between a purchase order and what the workshop could actually cut.
  3. Built the reference layer the function ran on: the supplier database and the stock labelling system used to identify and locate material.
  4. Modelled the ordering and inventory process so it could be handed over and run the same way by someone else.

Result

Continuity

Thirteen consecutive months, documented without a gap.

Ordering, supplier coordination and inventory movement ran unbroken from April 2021 to April 2022, held in a single continuous record.

Transferability

The function was left as artifacts, not as knowledge in one head.

A supplier database, a stock labelling system and a documented process model — so the ordering cycle could be picked up and run by someone else.
